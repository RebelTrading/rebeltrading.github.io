Our Philosophy

Built For Traders Who Wanted A Better Game

Rebel Trading was created to make simulated prop-style trading lower-cost, faster to understand, and more fun to practice.

Founder Story

Rebel Trading was founded by Ted Teske, a trader with over 10 years of market experience. After spending years around trading rooms, prop-style evaluations, challenge accounts, payout rules, and platform restrictions, Ted kept seeing the same problem: traders were paying too much money just to test whether they had a real edge.

Traditional prop firms can feel heavy. Big fees. Complex rules. Delayed feedback. Accounts that look exciting on the surface but become frustrating once traders realize how many hidden constraints are stacked against them. Rebel was built from that frustration. The goal was not to copy the old model. The goal was to strip it down, make it clearer, and turn it into something traders could actually practice with.

That is why Rebel Trading takes an arcade-first approach. We use simulated crypto perpetual markets, objective rules, XP rewards, and low-cost entry tickets to create a trading arena where users can build discipline without putting hundreds of dollars into a traditional evaluation. You choose a box, trade the sim, respect the rules, and see exactly where you stand.

Rebel is not pretending to be Wall Street. It is a skill-based trading simulator with prop-style pressure, arcade energy, and a direct feedback loop. Our belief is simple: traders should be able to get reps, test execution, and learn risk control without getting buried by expensive gates before they even start.

What We Believe
Lower Entry, More Reps Traders should be able to practice under real rules without risking large upfront evaluation fees.
Rules Should Be Visible The challenge should be hard because trading is hard, not because the rules are hidden or confusing.
Simulation Can Still Create Pressure Drawdown limits, trading days, best day rules, and margin guards create structure without live capital trading.
Rewards Should Feel Earned Rebel XP turns performance into a game-style reward path while keeping eligibility checks and manual review in place.
