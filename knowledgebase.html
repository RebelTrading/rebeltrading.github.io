Getting Started

KB-001 / Platform Overview

What is Rebel Trading Group?

Rebel Trading Group is a simulated micro crypto prop evaluation platform. Traders purchase a low-entry challenge ticket, trade in a browser-based simulated crypto perpetual environment, and can unlock a Sim Funded Rebel Stage after passing the one-step challenge.

The Rebel experience is built with an arcade-style progression layer, but the account rules, risk guardrails, dashboard tracking, and payout workflows are structured like a professional prop-style evaluation.

KB-002 / Challenge Tiers

Which challenge sizes are available?

Rebel currently offers three simulated challenge tiers:

  • Recruit Box: $1,000 simulated account.
  • Raider Box: $2,000 simulated account.
  • Rebel Box: $3,000 simulated account.

Each tier has its own starting balance, profit target, daily loss guard, trailing drawdown wall, and max position margin limit.

KB-003 / Account Access

How do traders create or access an account?

Traders can create an account through the Rebel membership page using email and password or Google sign-in. After signing in, the members dashboard loads account status, active challenge data, trade history, payout profile, and support access.

Challenge Rules

KB-004 / One-Step Path

Is there a Phase 2 evaluation?

No. Rebel uses a one-step challenge. Once a trader reaches the required profit target while respecting the rules, the account automatically transitions into the Sim Funded Rebel Stage.

There is no second evaluation stage, no extra activation fee after passing, and no manual unlock queue.

KB-005 / Profit Target

What is the Level 1 profit target?

The Rebel Challenge target is 10% of the selected simulated account balance. A $1,000 account has a $100 target, a $2,000 account has a $200 target, and a $3,000 account has a $300 target.

KB-006 / Daily Loss Guard

How does the daily loss safeguard work?

The daily loss safeguard limits how much the simulated account can lose from its starting balance threshold. If the account breaches the daily loss rule, the challenge can be marked failed and trading is locked for that account.

KB-007 / EOD Trailing Drawdown

How does the EOD trailing drawdown work?

The EOD trailing drawdown updates after the trading day closes, based on the account end-of-day high. It is not a tick-by-tick liquidation trap during open trades. If the account later falls below the EOD floor, the challenge can be marked failed.

KB-008 / Max Position Margin

What is the max position margin guard?

The max position margin guard limits how much of the simulated account can be allocated to a single position. The trade panel calculates notional value, margin allocated at 1:50 leverage, and remaining buying power before order entry.

If the selected contract size requires too much margin, the guard shows the position as over limit and blocks the order.

Trade Desk

KB-009 / Browser-Based Trading

Does the trade simulator work in the browser?

Yes. The Rebel trade desk is browser-based and works across desktop and mobile. Traders can access charts, market orders, limit orders, order book depth, time and sales, position tracking, and live account rule status without downloading software.

KB-010 / Supported Perpetuals

Which markets can traders simulate?

The trade desk supports simulated crypto perpetual markets. Current supported markets include BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, XLM, HYPE, DOGE, and BNB.

Prices and chart candles are sourced from live market data feeds, while all Rebel orders and balances remain simulated inside the evaluation environment.

KB-011 / Order Types

What order types are available?

The trade desk supports market orders and limit orders. Traders can also attach take profit and stop loss bracket parameters before entering a simulated position.

KB-012 / Contract Size And Margin

How does contract size affect margin?

The trade panel calculates notional value using asset price, selected contract size, and the asset multiplier. Margin allocated is calculated at 1:50 leverage. Higher-priced assets usually hit margin limits with fewer contracts, while lower-priced assets allow larger contract counts.

Sim Funded

KB-013 / Sim Funded Unlock

What happens after passing Level 1?

After a trader passes the Level 1 challenge, the system automatically unlocks the Sim Funded Rebel Stage. The account resets to the original selected balance size, the profit target becomes unlimited, and funded-stage payout tracking becomes active.

KB-014 / Speedrun Split

How does the Speedrun split work?

Standard Sim Funded accounts receive an 80% trader split. Traders who pass the Level 1 challenge within the Speedrun window can unlock a boosted 90% funded-stage payout split.

Payouts

KB-015 / Payout Eligibility

When can a trader request a payout?

Payouts are only available from Sim Funded profits. Challenge-stage profits unlock funded access but are not directly withdrawable.

To request a payout, the account must be in Sim Funded status, profits must be above the required buffer floor, the request must meet the minimum request amount, and the funded trading day requirement must be satisfied.

KB-016 / Buffer Floor

What is the payout buffer?

The payout buffer is the starting balance plus 4.2%. Only funded-stage profit above that buffer can become withdrawable. Payout requests must also meet the minimum request threshold.

KB-017 / USDC Wallet Setup

How are payout wallets saved?

Traders save a USDC payout wallet inside the members dashboard. Rebel supports USDC payout routing across available checkout-style networks, including Solana, Ethereum, Base, Polygon, Arbitrum, and BNB.

Traders should always confirm that their wallet supports the selected payout network before submitting a request.

KB-018 / Payout Processing

How are payouts processed?

Payout requests enter manual review. Approved payout requests are processed in USDC, and the payout history table tracks request status, network, wallet, and transaction hash when available.

Accounts

KB-019 / Members Dashboard

What is available in the members dashboard?

The members dashboard displays account stage, equity, realized P&L, drawdown floor, daily loss buffer, trade history, payout eligibility, payout wallet setup, payout history, and support desk access.

KB-020 / Sign-In Options

Can traders sign in with Google?

Yes. Rebel supports Google sign-in and standard email/password sign-in. After login, the user receives a Rebel session token used by the members dashboard, trade desk, payout tools, and support desk.

Troubleshooting

KB-021 / No Active Challenge

Why does my dashboard say no active challenge?

This means the account is signed in but does not currently have an active challenge. The trader should purchase a challenge or confirm that payment processing completed successfully.

KB-022 / Trading Locked

Why is trading locked?

Trading can lock if the account failed a risk rule, if there is no active challenge, or if the session is invalid. Failed accounts cannot continue trading and require a new challenge to restart the path.

KB-023 / Chart Or Price Feed Issues

What should traders do if chart data does not load?

Traders should refresh the browser, confirm they are signed in, and verify their internet connection. If the issue continues, they should contact support with the asset, browser, device type, and approximate time of the issue.
