How Rebel Payouts Work

Rebel Challenge profits unlock access. Sim Funded profits unlock payouts.

The Rebel Challenge is your one-step evaluation. It does not produce a direct payout. When you hit the 10% challenge target while respecting the risk rules, your account transitions into the Sim Funded Rebel Stage.

Once you are Sim Funded, your account resets to your selected balance size, profit potential becomes unlimited, and payouts become available from funded-stage profits above the required account buffer.

STEP 01

Pass The Rebel Challenge

Select your account tier, launch the trading desk, and hit the 10% evaluation target. Challenge rules include a 5% max daily loss and a 10% EOD trailing drawdown.

Passing the challenge unlocks the Sim Funded Rebel Stage. Evaluation-stage profits are not paid out directly.

STEP 02

Enter Sim Funded Stage

Your account resets to the original selected balance size. Profit potential becomes unlimited, the 5% daily loss rule remains active, and the EOD trailing drawdown tightens to 8%.

Your payout split is 80% by default. If you earned Speedrun status by passing the challenge within 48 hours or first 50 trades, your funded-stage split becomes 90%.

STEP 03

Save Your USDC Wallet

Add your USDC payout wallet inside the members dashboard. Rebel supports USDC payout routing across the same checkout networks used for entry payments.

Always confirm your wallet supports the selected network before submitting a payout request.

The Sim Funded Payout Structure

Simple payout access, retained account buffer, and USDC processing within 24 hours after approval.

Base Split

80%

Standard Sim Funded accounts receive 80% of approved funded-stage payout requests.

Speedrun Split

90%

Pass the Rebel Challenge within 48 hours or first 50 trades to unlock a boosted 90% funded-stage payout split.

Payout Buffer

4.2%

Payouts are available only on funded-stage profits above the starting balance plus the 4.2% account buffer.

Minimum Request

$25

Payout requests must be at least $25 USDC after the account has cleared the required buffer line.

Frequency

5 Days

Sim Funded traders may request payouts every 5 trading days, subject to account status and risk compliance.

Processing

24H

Approved payout requests are processed manually in USDC within 24 hours while Rebel is in early access.
