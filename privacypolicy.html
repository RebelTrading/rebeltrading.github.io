Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 28, 2026

Rebel Trading Group operates a simulated crypto prop trading challenge and dashboard experience. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect account details such as name, display name, email address, login credentials, selected challenge tier, dashboard activity, trade simulation records, payout wallet information, support messages, and basic technical data such as browser, device, IP address, and usage logs.

2. Payment And Wallet Information

Crypto checkout and payment activity may be processed through third-party payment or checkout providers. Rebel does not store private keys or seed phrases. If you submit a payout wallet address, we use it only for payout review, payout processing, fraud prevention, and account support.

3. How We Use Information

  • To create and manage your Rebel account.
  • To activate simulated challenges and display dashboard metrics.
  • To track simulated trades, challenge status, payout eligibility, and account rules.
  • To process support requests and communicate with you.
  • To protect the platform from fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, or rule violations.
  • To improve site performance, user experience, and platform reliability.

4. How We Share Information

We may share limited information with service providers that help operate Rebel, including hosting, analytics, authentication, payment checkout, email, support, database, and security tools. We may also disclose information if required by law, to enforce our terms, or to protect Rebel, users, or the platform.

5. We Do Not Sell Your Personal Information

Rebel does not sell your personal information. We also do not ask for or store your crypto private keys, seed phrases, or exchange login credentials.

6. Cookies And Local Storage

We may use cookies, browser storage, and local storage to keep you logged in, remember session state, improve dashboard behavior, and support platform security. You can control some browser storage through your browser settings, but disabling it may break login or dashboard features.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect account data. No online system is perfectly secure, so users should use strong passwords, protect their devices, and never share wallet private keys or seed phrases.

8. Data Retention

We keep information as long as needed to operate Rebel, maintain account records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and enforce our terms. We may retain certain transaction, challenge, payout, and support records for internal audit and compliance purposes.

9. Your Choices

You may contact us to request account assistance, correction of inaccurate account information, or deletion review. Some records may need to be retained for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, accounting, or legal reasons.

10. Children

Rebel is not intended for children. Users must meet the eligibility requirements stated in our Terms of Use.

11. Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Rebel evolves. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

12. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to: rebeltradinggroup@proton.me
