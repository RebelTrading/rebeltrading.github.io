  • Funded-stage trades are tracked separately from evaluation trades.
  • Payout eligibility begins only from funded-stage profit above the buffer floor.

8. Payout Terms

Payouts are available only during the Sim Funded Rebel Stage and only from eligible simulated funded-stage profits.

  • Standard Split: 80% of approved eligible funded-stage profits.
  • Speedrun Split: 90% of approved eligible funded-stage profits if the user qualified by passing Level 1 within 48 hours.
  • Payout Buffer: Account equity must remain above the starting balance plus 5%.
  • Minimum Request: Users must have at least $25 above the buffer floor to request payout.
  • Trading Day Requirement: Users must complete 5 funded trading days before a payout request is eligible.
  • Funded Trade Requirement: Users must close at least 10 funded-stage trades before a payout request is eligible.
  • Funded Best Day Rule: The best profitable funded-stage trading day may not account for more than 50% of total positive funded-stage profit. If your best day is above 50%, your account does not fail, but you must keep trading until total positive profit brings that day back to 50% or less.
  • Duplicate Request Block: Users may not submit a new payout request while another request is pending manual review.
  • Manual Review: All payout requests are subject to review before approval.
  • Settlement Rail: Approved payouts are intended to be sent in USDC to the wallet address provided by the user.

When a payout is approved and marked paid, the gross requested profit amount is deducted from the simulated account equity. The user receives the approved split percentage of that gross amount.

Example: if a user requests $25 of eligible funded-stage profit with a 90% split, the user may receive $22.50 USDC, and the simulated account equity is reduced by the full $25 gross request.
