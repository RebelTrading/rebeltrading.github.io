Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about Rebel Challenges, Sim Funded accounts, payouts, leverage, and rule enforcement.
What is Rebel Trading Group?

Rebel Trading Group is a simulated micro crypto prop trading platform. Traders buy a low-cost challenge ticket, trade BTC, ETH, SOL, and XRP perpetual simulations, and can unlock a Sim Funded Rebel Stage after passing the one-step challenge.

Is there a Phase 2 evaluation?

No. Rebel uses a one-step challenge. Once you hit the 10% target while respecting the rules, your account automatically moves into the Sim Funded Rebel Stage. No second evaluation, no activation fee, and no monthly subscription.

What can I trade?

The Rebel trade desk currently focuses on high-liquidity crypto perpetual simulations: BTC, ETH, SOL, and XRP. The platform uses a browser-based custom trade UI with charting, indicators, order book depth, market orders, and limit orders.

How does the EOD trailing drawdown work?

The EOD trailing drawdown updates after the trading day closes, based on your end-of-day account high. It is not designed as a tick-by-tick liquidation trap. If your account later falls below the EOD floor, the challenge can be marked failed.

How do funded-stage payouts work?

After you enter the Sim Funded Rebel Stage, payout requests become available only from funded-stage profits above the 4.2% buffer floor. You must also meet the $25 minimum request and complete 5 funded trading days before requesting a USDC payout.

What is the payout split?

Standard Sim Funded accounts receive an 80% trader split. Traders who clear Level 1 within 48 hours unlock a 90% funded split. The 90% split is tied to the 48-hour Speedrun condition only.

Is this live trading with real capital?

No. Rebel is a simulated trading and evaluation environment. Trades are simulated, account balances are simulated, and payouts are based on approved simulated performance according to Rebel rules and payout terms.
