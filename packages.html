Rules That Matter

Risk Guard Breakdown

The rules are built to protect the sim environment while keeping the path understandable. Your dashboard and trade desk track the important metrics as you trade.

5% Daily Loss

The intraday drawdown guard stays active through the challenge and funded-stage path.

10% Challenge EOD Trail

The Level 1 EOD trailing drawdown is 10% of the selected simulated account size.

8% Funded EOD Trail

After passing into Sim Funded, the EOD trailing drawdown tightens to 8% of the selected account size.

Position Margin Guard

Recruit allows 25%, Raider allows 30%, and Rebel allows 35% max position margin exposure.

Speedrun Boost

Clear Level 1 within the Speedrun window to unlock a 90% funded split instead of the standard 80% split.

No Phase 2

Rebel uses a one-step challenge. Pass Level 1 and the Sim Funded stage unlocks automatically.

USDC Payout Rails

Payout requests are designed around approved funded-stage profits and stablecoin settlement.
